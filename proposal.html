GlassDollar Mobile
A companion for the buyer on the move.

A focused Expo app over your existing backend, built for the senior, budget-holding user who works from their phone at events and between meetings, not at a desk.

Prepared by Özgür Celebi For Stephen · GlassDollar Target Mobile support by end of June 2026
Expo · React Native iOS · Android · Web Your Auth0 + Hasura · no backend work Read-mostly · event-first
In one line

A front-end-only Expo app that gives your on-the-go buyers the few journeys that matter on a phone: find a startup, save it, hand them a QR, glance at a project. Shipped as an installable hybrid app over the same Auth0 + Hasura the web product already runs on.

The insight it's built on

The buyer isn't the daily user

Direction taken straight from the kickoff call. This is what reorders the scope away from "shrink the desktop app."

Two-sided reality

The budget owners (C-level) who pay for it use it on the move. The teams they pay for (innovation managers) work at the desk all day. The app serves the buyer, not the whole desktop product shrunk down.

The hero moment is events

"I'm at a fair, I want to find a startup and get them into my database." Search → profile → save to a list, and show your event QR so startups self-submit their pitch into your account.

The commitment

You've committed mobile support to a customer for end of June. As you described it, that doesn't hinge on an App Store listing. A polished hybrid experience meets it.

But not web-only

You want a real mobile experience. And the current desktop SPA already breaks at phone width (verified), so retrofitting responsive is neither cheap nor good.

Approach

Why Expo

One React codebase for iOS, Android, and web, reusing your stack and hedging the App-Store / corporate-IT-clearance risk.

OptionVerdict
Expo (React Native) ✓ chosenOne codebase → iOS + Android + web. Reuses Auth0 + Hasura + PostHog/Sentry. Ships hybrid now, submits to stores later. Fast: reuses your existing components for a faithful, mobile-adapted UI.
Native (Swift/Kotlin) ✗Two codebases, slower, no reuse. Can't hit end-of-June.
Web-only / home-screen bookmark ✗Poor auth + UX, you don't want it, and the desktop SPA already breaks on phones.
Backend, confirmed from the live demo and ready to consume: Auth = Auth0 with Organizations (multi-tenant), data = Hasura GraphQL, analytics = PostHog, errors = Sentry. The Expo app is a thin native client over the same API the web app uses. I don't touch the backend.
MVP · in scope

The core journeys, screen by screen

Each mobile screen is reduced from a real screen in your product, so this is grounded, not invented.

1

Sign in

Auth0, tenant-aware, no onboarding (existing customers). Biometric unlock optional.

↳ Auth0 login
2

Instant search

Keyword → grouped results: companies, lists, projects. Tap a company → profile. Synchronous and simple, the right fit for finding a known startup at an event.

↳ ⌘K omniboxthe everyday search
3

Startup profile

Aggregated profile (Crunchbase/CB Insights/PitchBook): description, customers, investors, similar, linked projects + status. Add to list · Share.

↳ profile panel
4

QR capture

Show a Challenge's QR full-screen at events. Startups scan it to self-submit their pitch, landing as applications in your account.

↳ Open Challenges → Share → QRevent hero feature
5

Lists / Library

"Playlist" experience: browse lists, open a list as a scrollable stack of startup cards, add to list, create a list, Share.

↳ Lists + list matrix
6

Read-only project

Snapshot: AI summary, stage timeline, status (e.g. At Risk), responsible person. Can comment, cannot edit or run.

↳ PoC detail
7

Account

Minimal profile/settings, tenant indicator, sign-out.

↳ new
QR is lower-risk than it looks: the QR-at-events feature already exists in the web product (Open Challenges → Share → QR, with Download). Mobile mainly re-presents it for scanning and shows incoming applications.
